Plant Types

Choose the Right Configuration

Pick based on your grid availability, backup need and savings goal.

On-Grid

Grid-connected with net metering.

  • No batteries needed
  • Lowest cost per unit
  • Fastest payback
  • Best for homes and businesses with a stable grid
Most Flexible

Hybrid

Solar + battery + grid together.

  • Backup during outages
  • Peak-load shaving
  • Smart energy management
  • Best for critical loads and high tariffs

Off-Grid

Fully independent, battery-backed.

  • No utility connection required
  • Battery storage included
  • Reliable round-the-clock power
  • Best for remote and rural sites

FAQ

Everything You Need to Know

Quick answers on plant types, savings, installation and upkeep.

On-Grid plants exchange power with the utility through net metering and need no batteries. Hybrid plants add battery storage so critical loads stay on during outages. Off-Grid plants run on solar and batteries alone, which suits remote sites.

Savings depend on your tariff, consumption and system size. After a site survey we share an estimated generation, unit-cost saving and payback period so you can decide with real numbers.

Rooftop systems are usually installed within a few weeks of approvals and material delivery. Larger industrial and ground-mounted plants follow a project schedule agreed after design.
