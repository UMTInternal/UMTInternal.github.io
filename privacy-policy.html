Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect the information you share with Charzee.

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Charzee EV Solutions ("Charzee", "we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you visit this website or contact us, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that you choose to give us, and a small amount of technical information needed to deliver the website.

  • Contact and enquiry details — your name, phone number, email address, subject and message when you submit the contact form or request a quote.
  • Communication records — messages you send us by email, phone or WhatsApp, and the details you share in them.
  • Technical information — standard data your browser sends when loading any website, such as IP address, browser type and device type. This is processed by our hosting and content-delivery providers.

We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your enquiries, quotes and support requests.
  • To plan, supply, install and maintain the products and services you ask about.
  • To send you information you have requested, such as brochures and datasheets.
  • To keep the website secure and working properly.
  • To meet our legal, tax and regulatory obligations.

We process your personal data on the basis of your consent, to take steps you request before entering into a contract, and where required by law, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

3. Third-Party Services

This website relies on a few trusted third-party services. Each handles data under its own privacy policy:

  • EmailJS — delivers contact-form submissions to our inbox.
  • Google Maps — shows our location on the contact page.
  • Google Fonts, jsDelivr and unpkg — serve fonts, icons and styling libraries.
  • WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn — only when you choose to follow a link to them.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only:

  • With installation partners or service providers who need it to fulfil your request, under confidentiality obligations.
  • When required by law, court order or a government authority.
  • To protect the rights, property or safety of Charzee, our customers or others.

5. Data Retention and Security

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

6. Your Rights

You can ask us to access, correct, update or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw consent you have given. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it was last changed.
